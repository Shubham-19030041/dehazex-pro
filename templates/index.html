About DeHazeX Pro

Deep-learning image dehazing comparing AOD-Net (CNN), Attention U-Net (proposed hybrid), and DehazeGAN, evaluated on the RESIDE benchmark using PSNR, SSIM, Entropy, Edge Preservation and inference time. Also includes the classical Dark Channel Prior (DCP) method as a zero-training baseline for comparison.

Built with TensorFlow + Flask.
